## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, relaxed creative agency aesthetic that merges editorial precision with architectural clarity. It projects effortless confidence, cultural savvy, and bespoke craft. The target audience comprises ambitious brand leaders, founders, and creative directors seeking top-tier agency design without visual clutter or corporate sterility.

The visual style unites contemporary minimalism with generous, organic softness:
- Crisp, warm off-white canvases ground the experience, eliminating harsh digital glare.
- Pristine typography delivers effortless scannability and high-end editorial rhythm.
- Tactile, large-radius geometry softens layouts, projecting warmth and approachability.
- Vibrant, disciplined accent touches (deep crimson and electric cyan) create decisive focal moments without diluting visual composure.

## Colors

The palette balances serene, warm neutrals with assertive, high-personality accents.

- **Primary (`#A01318`)**: Deep crimson used for hero calls to action, high-priority status states, active markers, and signature brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#30B4D7`)**: Vivid electric cyan reserved for creative badges, interactive highlights, subtle category tags, and secondary visual moments.
- **Tertiary / Ink (`#101014`)**: Saturated charcoal black for primary text, iconography, and high-contrast structural framing. Never use pure `#000000`.
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBF9`)**: Warm architectural off-white that acts as the default page background.
- **Surface Containers**:
  - `surface-default`: `#FFFFFF` for elevated foreground cards and dynamic floating modules.
  - `surface-muted`: `#F4F4F0` for structural divisions, list backgrounds, and inset containers.
  - `surface-subtle`: `#EFEFEA` for hover interactions and secondary toolbars.
- **Border / Rule Tone**: `#E5E5DE` at 1px width for whisper-soft separation when tonal differentiation needs extra definition.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples geometric presence with pristine readability.

- **Display & Headlines (`Epilogue`)**: Provides purposeful weight, architectural lines, and bold geometric personality. Large headlines use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and solid leading to give hero titles an authoritative, bespoke character.
- **Editorial Sub-headings & Pullquotes**: Set in light weights (300) of `Epilogue` to introduce an airy, relaxed studio tone that complements heavy display titles.
- **Body & Functional UI (`DM Sans`)**: Provides crisp, low-contrast neutral clarity for long-form case studies, metadata, and form components.
- **Scale Behavior**: Large headlines step down smoothly between desktop and mobile break points to preserve editorial weight without awkward wrapping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an open, generous rhythm that gives creative artifacts and content space to breathe.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width `1440px`), 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Canvas Margins**: Deep outer margins (`margin`: `3rem`) provide a framed gallery effect on large screens, collapsing to `1.25rem` on mobile.
- **Section Rhythm**: Inter-section gaps rely on generous scales (`4rem` to `7rem`), celebrating negative space rather than filling every viewport zone.
- **Flow & Reflow**: Multi-column case study cards flow into full-width stacked layouts on screens under `768px`. Navigation transforms from a lightweight desktop bar into a full-bleed modal overlay.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through gentle tonal layers and ambient diffusion rather than artificial 3D constructs.

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is created primarily through color shifts: the `#FBFBF9` base canvas holds `#FFFFFF` primary cards, accented by inset modules in `#F4F4F0`.
- **Soft Ambient Shadows**: Floating elements (such as elevated project cards, dropdowns, and floating navigation bars) use an ultra-diffused, warm shadow:
  - *Resting Card*: `0 8px 30px rgba(16, 16, 20, 0.04)`
  - *Hovered Card / Floating Nav*: `0 16px 40px rgba(16, 16, 20, 0.07)`
  - *Active Modals*: `0 24px 60px rgba(16, 16, 20, 0.10)`
- **Edge Definition**: A whisper-thin outline (`1px solid #E5E5DE`) is paired with soft ambient shadows to keep edges crisp on high-DPI displays.
- **Backdrop Diffusion**: Sticky headers and popovers utilize backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over an 85% opacity surface for modern atmospheric transparency.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes fluid, friendly sophistication using generous radii.

- **Cards & Case Study Modules**: Feature pronounced corners between `1.25rem` (20px) and `1.75rem` (28px). These soft contours counter-balance the sharp, crisp charcoal typography.
- **Interactive Pills**: Interactive buttons, tags, chips, and utility badges utilize pill contours (`9999px`), creating tactile, approachable touchpoints.
- **Input Fields & Insets**: Form inputs and nested containers settle at `1rem` (16px) radius, maintaining structural harmony with parent cards.
- **Media Containers**: Portfolio imagery and video embeds mirror the parent card radius (`1.25rem`–`1.75rem`), with overflow strictly hidden to maintain clean silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep crimson (`#A01318`) fill, crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) text, pill-shaped radius (`9999px`), horizontal padding `1.75rem`, vertical padding `0.875rem`. Hover state introduces a smooth translateY(-1px) and ambient shadow lift.
- **Secondary / Action**: Vibrant cyan blue (`#30B4D7`) fill with white (`#FFFFFF`) text for alternative creative conversions.
- **Ghost / Minimalist**: Transparent background with `#101014` text and a subtle `#E5E5DE` border. On hover, transitions to `#F4F4F0` surface fill.
- **Text Link**: Charcoal text with an animated bottom highlight in deep crimson or vibrant cyan.

### Cards & Project Tiles
- **Default Card**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background, `1.5rem` (24px) border radius, `1px solid #E5E5DE`, and resting ambient shadow. Padding is generous: `2rem` to `2.5rem`.
- **Muted Card**: `#F4F4F0` fill with no border, ideal for secondary quotes, studio stats, and metadata panels.
- **Interactive State**: Lifts by `4px` on hover with shadow expansion, creating an inviting, tactile feel.

### Chips & Tags
- **Category Badge**: Pill-shaped with `#F4F4F0` background, `#101014` label text, `0.75rem` font size, and `0.375rem 0.875rem` padding.
- **Accent Badge**: Electric cyan tint (`#30B4D7` at 12% opacity) background with rich `#0F748E` text for standout creative tags.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface set to `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F4F0` with `1rem` corner rounding.
- Border is `1px solid #E5E5DE`.
- Focus state activates a precise `2px` focus ring in `#101014` or deep red (`#A01318`), avoiding default browser glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Subtle rounded square (`6px`) for checkboxes; full circle for radios.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px solid #101014`.
- Selected: Solid `#A01318` fill with crisp white icon checkmark.

### Navigation Header
- Floating dock format: elevated off-white pill (`#FFFFFF` with 90% opacity, backdrop blur), anchored at the top of the viewport with uncluttered links, generous spacing, and a primary CTA pill.
